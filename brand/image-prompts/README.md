# PliegoCSS generated-image prompts

These specifications are the canonical GPT Image 2 art direction for the
website and launch materials. They are prompts, not evidence that an image has
already been generated.

Final images must be reviewed against the brandbook, saved beside a prompt
receipt, and derived into web formats without overwriting their masters.

| Prompt | Intended output | Website role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01-cascade-chamber.md` | 16:9 master | Homepage editorial hero field |
| `02-semantic-fold.md` | 4:3 master | Compiler narrative |
| `03-evidence-archive.md` | 4:3 master | Evidence and provenance |
| `04-material-studies.md` | square series | Brand and social crops |

Generation requirements:

- Model: GPT Image 2 through DigitalOcean Serverless Inference, recorded by
  its exact catalog identifier `openai-gpt-image-2`.
- Final quality: high.
- No in-image text, code, UI, logo, or watermark.
- Preserve Carbon, Paper, Cobalt, and Cyan roles.
- Keep Coral and Amber absent unless the subject explicitly represents a
  diagnostic state.
- Record the exact final prompt and generation settings with each master.
