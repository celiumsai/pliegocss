# Community code of conduct

PliegoCSS is built for exacting technical work. Critique may be direct;
treatment of people must remain respectful.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas, evidence, and behavior without attacking the person.
- Make room for different backgrounds, experience, and communication styles.
- Give actionable feedback and receive correction without retaliation.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and disclosure boundaries.
- Stop behavior when a maintainer or participant says it is unwelcome.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
humiliation, doxxing, sustained disruption, impersonation, and publishing
private communications without permission are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@pliegocss.dev` with relevant links,
dates, and context. Maintainers may remove content, warn, limit participation,
temporarily suspend access, or permanently exclude a participant according to
severity and pattern. Conflicts of interest will be disclosed when practical.

This policy applies to repository activity, documentation, community channels,
events, and direct project-related communication.
